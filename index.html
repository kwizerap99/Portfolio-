About the Artist

Kwizera Patrick

Kwizera Patrick is an award-winning multidisciplinary visual artist based in Kigali, Rwanda, whose figurative acrylic portraiture explores identity, mental health, and everyday African experiences through expressive color, symbolism, and vivid backdrops.

A founding member of Pamoja Art, he earned global recognition as a Top 150 Nominee in the High Art Contest (2022) and a Semifinalist in Art Rwanda Ubuhanzi (2022), leading to his debut solo exhibition, The Blossom That Sway, in 2024. He holds a diploma from the prestigious Nyundo School of Arts and a certificate from the ALU School of Business via the SoCreative Entrepreneurship Program.
